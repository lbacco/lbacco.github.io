        My research focuses on **reliable and data-efficient AI for biomedical applications**, with particular emphasis on **natural language processing, large language models, multimodal learning, explainability, and clinical decision support**.

        A complete and up-to-date list is also available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Aw4cEzMAAAAJ). You can also browse the [complete publication archive](/all-publications/).

        _Last updated: August 2026._

        ## Selected Journal Publications

        Giaccone, P., D'Antoni, F., **Bacco, L.**, Russo, F., Papalia, G.F., Vadalà, G., Papalia, R., Pecchia, L., Denaro, V., Merone, M.  
        **[Artificial intelligence based intervertebral discs grading: From unsupervised detection to explainable Pfirrmann classification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2026.110177)**  
        _Biomedical Signal Processing and Control_, 2026.  
        → Builds an end-to-end lumbar MRI pipeline combining unsupervised disc detection, eight-level Pfirrmann grading, visual explanations, and confidence-aware rejection.

        Piperno, R., **Bacco, L.**, Dell'Orletta, F., Merone, M., Pecchia, L.  
        **[Cross-Lingual Distillation for Domain Knowledge Transfer with Sentence Transformers](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2025.113079)**  
        _Knowledge-Based Systems_, 2025.  
        → Transfers biomedical domain and task knowledge from English to lower-resource languages through cross-lingual sentence-embedding distillation.

        Bonfigli, A., **Bacco, L.**, Pecchia, L., Merone, M., Dell'Orletta, F.  
        **[Efficient Multi-Task Learning with Instance Selection for Biomedical NLP](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2025.110050)**  
        _Computers in Biology and Medicine_, 2025.  
        → Combines multi-task learning and instance selection to reduce biomedical NLP training data while preserving competitive performance.

        Bonfigli, A., **Bacco, L.**, Merone, M., Dell'Orletta, F.  
        **[From Pre-training to Fine-tuning: An In-depth Analysis of Large Language Models in the Biomedical Domain](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artmed.2024.103003)**  
        _Artificial Intelligence in Medicine_, 2024.  
        → Systematically compares encoder and decoder language models, domain adaptation, fine-tuning data size, and internal representations in biomedical NLP.

        **Bacco, L.**, Dell'Orletta, F., Lai, H., Merone, M., Nissim, M.  
        **[A Text Style Transfer System for Reducing the Physician-Patient Expertise Gap: An Analysis with Automatic and Human Evaluations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2023.120874)**  
        _Expert Systems with Applications_, 2023.  
        → Uses sentence embeddings to mine pseudo-parallel expert-lay data and train a medical text simplification system evaluated with both automatic and human assessments.

        **Bacco, L.**, Minnema, G., Caselli, T., Dell'Orletta, F., Merone, M., Nissim, M.  
        **[On the Instability of Further Pre-Training: Does a Single Sentence Matter to BERT?](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlp.2023.100037)**  
        _Natural Language Processing Journal_, 2023.  
        → Shows that even one sentence of additional BERT pre-training can change downstream fine-tuning trajectories and performance, exposing model-instability issues.

        ## Selected Conference Publications

        Bonfigli, A., Di Giacinto, A.M., Giordani, F., Pecchia, L., Merone, M., **Bacco, L.**  
        **A Multimodal Wearable Framework for Predicting Epileptic Seizures**  
        _24th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (AIME)_, 2026.  
        → Investigates multimodal wearable data for predicting epileptic seizures.

        Piperno, R., **Bacco, L.**, Papalia, G.F., Russo, F., Maltese, G., Vadalà, G., Papalia, R., Pecchia, L., Merone, M.  
        **[Multi-View Lumbar MRI for Therapy-Oriented Decision Support System in Low Back Pain](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-30813-9_79)**  
        _24th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (AIME)_, 2026.  
        → Explores multi-view lumbar MRI as a basis for therapy-oriented decision support in low back pain.

        Piperno, R., Bonfigli, A., Dell'Orletta, F., Pecchia, L., Merone, M., **Bacco, L.**  
        **[Doctor, Is That You? Evaluating Large Language Models on Italy's Medical School Entrance Exams](https://aclanthology.org/2025.clicit-1.85/)**  
        _11th Italian Conference on Computational Linguistics (CLiC-it)_, 2025.  
        → Benchmarks open-weight language models on more than 3,300 real Italian medical-school entrance questions, showing a clear advantage for large multilingual models over Italian-only models.

        Bonfigli, A., Piperno, R., **Bacco, L.**, Dell'Orletta, F., Brunato, D., Crispino, F., Papalia, G.F., Russo, F., Vadalà, G., Papalia, R., Merone, M., Pecchia, L.  
        **[A Novel Real-World Dataset of Italian Clinical Notes for NLP-based Decision Support in Low Back Pain Treatment](https://aclanthology.org/2025.clicit-1.12/)**  
        _11th Italian Conference on Computational Linguistics (CLiC-it)_, 2025.  
        → Releases an annotated Italian low-back-pain dataset of 176 patients with multimodal clinical information and benchmarks classical NLP against a fine-tuned Italian BERT model.
